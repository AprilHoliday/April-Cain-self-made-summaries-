> [!NOTE]
> 
> Move with stealth and act with sleight of hand. Sneak, lock pick, steal 
> 
> ==Covert== is used when doing something silently or unseen, or undertaking activities usually associated with covert action, like pickpocketing or breaking locks. It’s not particularly fast and can’t be done in a hurry. If your exorcist is quiet, stealthy, or light on their feet, they probably have good covert. You might use it when: 
> 
> • avoiding the attention of a flesh eating monster 
> 
> • lifting the keys off a security guard 
> 
> • carefully avoiding alerting the web of fibrous nerve matter on the floor 
> 
> • hacking into a computer (but interfacing is probably better)
